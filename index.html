What is Nigeria?

Nigeria is a country in West Africa known for its rich cultural diversity, economic potential, and vibrant history. It is the most populous country in Africa and has a diverse landscape ranging from coastal areas to savannahs and plateaus.

Key Features of Nigeria

  • Geography: Bordered by Benin, Niger, Chad, and Cameroon. Has a coastline along the Atlantic Ocean (Gulf of Guinea).Features include forests, savannahs, rivers (Niger and Benue), and plateaus
  • Population and Ethnic Groups:Over 200 million people, making it the most populous country in Africa, with Over 250 ethnic groups and 500 languages. Major ethnic groups include Hausa/Fulani, Yoruba, and Igbo
  • Economy:One of Africa’s largest economies,Rich in natural resources, especially crude oil and natural gas. Other sectors include agriculture, trade, telecommunications, and entertainment
  • Culture:Known for rich traditions, music, festivals, and food. Nollywood, Nigeria’s film industry, is one of the largest in the world. Popular music styles include Afrobeats, highlife, and traditional music
  • Government and Administration:Federal Republic with 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). Governed by an elected President and a bicameral legislature (Senate and House of Representatives)
